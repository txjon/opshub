Pre-order SOP

How pre-orders move through OpsHub from idea to fulfillment. Each phase has one owner — when a phase ends, ownership hands off to the next person. The page you act in is on the right.

Who does what

Taylor
Scopes the pre-order with the client. Owns Planning. Sets up products, dates, target ship.
Abigail
Owns Shopify. Builds product listings, marks them ready, fields customer service. Also handles Shopify inventory entry on Intake.
Drake / Labs
Owns production. Receives the Labs job once the pre-order is pushed, manages costing, POs, decorator handoff.
Warehouse + ShipStation
Warehouse receives goods at HPD and confirms qtys. ShipStation handles daily fulfillment of customer orders.

Lifecycle at a glance

Seven phases, end to end. Each has a status chip in OpsHub.

PHASE 1 Planning Planning
Owner: Taylor · with Drake + client

Trigger

Client agrees to do a pre-order. Initial scoping conversation has happened.

What Taylor does in OpsHub

  1. Go to /ecomm → Pre-orders tab → + New Ecomm Project
  2. Fill in: Project name (e.g. "FOG May Pre-order"), Client, Mode = Pre-order, platform (Shopify), and the store URL if known.
  3. Open the new pre-order. On the detail page:
    • Set Opens + Closes dates from the client agreement.
    • Set Target ship date using the quick buttons (3w / 4w / 5w / 6w from close).
    • Set Buffer % (typically 5–7 — covers damage and sample pulls).
    • Add each Product: name, blank vendor/SKU, sizes, retail price.
  4. When all products + dates are entered, click "Hand off to Abigail (Build in Shopify)" at the top of the page.

Status moves to

Building in Shopify — Abigail picks up.

↓
PHASE 2 Building in Shopify Building in Shopify
Owner: Abigail

Trigger

Taylor flipped the pre-order to Building.

What Abigail does

  1. Open the pre-order on /ecomm → click into it.
  2. For each product listed, build the matching product in the client's Shopify store using the name, blank info, sizes, and retail price from OpsHub.
  3. When a Shopify product is live, tick the checkbox next to that product in OpsHub. It marks built with a timestamp.
  4. When all products are built (the page shows N/N · Shopify build in green), click "Mark pre-order open".

Status moves to

Open · live — customers can buy.

↓
PHASE 3 Open · live Open · live
Owner: Abigail (customer service) · automatic countdown to close

What's happening

The pre-order is selling in Shopify. Customers are placing orders. Nobody touches OpsHub other than Abigail answering questions ("When does it close?", "When will it ship?").

Customer-facing answers come from OpsHub

  • "When does the pre-order close?" → the Closes date on the pre-order.
  • "When will my order ship?" → the Target ship date until production reveals per-item ETAs.
  • For deeper status (after the order is in production), per-item ETAs show up on the linked Labs job once Drake pushes it.

Status moves to

Closed · pending push when the close date passes and the pre-order is closed in Shopify. Click "Close pre-order" in OpsHub.

↓
PHASE 4 Closed · pending push Closed · pending push
Owner: Abigail (with Drake reviewing)

Trigger

Pre-order closed in Shopify. Sold qtys are ready to pull.

What Abigail does

  1. Pull the Shopify sold-qty report per variant.
  2. In OpsHub, on the pre-order detail page, click "Push to production".
  3. The wizard opens. For each product:
    • Type the sold qty per size into the grid.
    • Buffer % is pre-filled from the pre-order. The wizard shows the calculated total to print per variant ( ceil(sold × (1 + buffer/100)) ).
  4. Confirm total at the bottom. Click "Create Labs job · N units".
  5. OpsHub spawns the Labs project, pre-loaded with items + buy sheet lines, and opens it in a new tab for Drake.

Status moves to

Producing — Drake takes over.

↓
PHASE 5 Producing Producing
Owner: Drake + Labs · standard production flow

What happens

The Labs job created from the push runs through the normal production pipeline:

  • Costing → Quote → Blanks ordered → POs to decorators → Items in production → Shipped from decorator → Received at HPD.
  • Drake sets per-item client ETAs on the Production tab as info firms up. These flow back to Abigail's customer-service answers automatically.
  • Throughout, the pre-order on /ecomm shows the linked Labs job so anyone can jump over.

Status moves to

Fulfilling when items hit HPD and Abigail has keyed inventory into Shopify (the Intake tab on /ecomm).

↓
PHASE 6 Fulfilling Fulfilling
Owner: ShipStation · warehouse picks/packs · OpsHub is read-only

What happens

ShipStation pulls Shopify orders and the warehouse picks/packs daily. OpsHub does not manage daily fulfillment — it just shows what landed and when.

Where the team works

  • Warehouse: ShipStation for daily picks. The /receiving tab in OpsHub shows fresh inventory marked "✓ Live in Shopify — shelve" for 48 hours after Abigail's entry, so they know what just arrived.
  • Abigail: Customer service. Per-item ETAs from the Labs job answer "where's my order?" until tracking exists in ShipStation.

Status moves to

Complete when all customer orders have shipped (manual mark once the pre-order is wrapped).

↓
PHASE 7 Complete Complete
Owner: Manager review

Reconciliation (coming soon as a built-in view)

At end-of-fulfillment, the pre-order should reconcile:

  • Printed N (from the Labs job)
  • Sold X (from Shopify report)
  • Shipped X (from ShipStation)
  • Expected leftover Y = N − X (the buffer is the cushion)

Warehouse compares actual count to expected leftover. Variance flags are investigated.

Common questions

What if the pre-order opens before all products are built?

The "Mark pre-order open" button is locked until every product is ticked as Built in Shopify. Hover the disabled button to see what's missing.

What if a customer asks "where's my order" while production is still running?

Abigail's answer comes from the per-item ETA on the Labs job (set on the Production tab) — not the original Target ship date. Per-item ETAs update as production firms up.

What if the Shopify report has a variant we didn't plan for?

The push wizard only knows about products listed on the pre-order. If a surprise variant shows up, add the product back on the pre-order detail page first, then re-open the push wizard.

What if zero units sold for a particular product?

The push wizard skips products with zero total qty. The Labs job won't have that item — saves the production team from chasing phantom inventory.

Can a pre-order be edited after it's been pushed to production?

Pre-order metadata (dates, notes) can still be edited. Products are locked once the Labs job is spawned — production-side changes happen on the Labs job, not the pre-order.
