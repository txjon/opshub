01 · The role

You bring the vision, we'll help bring it home.

You show up with finished artwork or a direction you want to chase. From there we handle the whole machine: complete product sourcing, optimizing your Shopify storefront, warehousing, and shipping every order.

We don't design from nothing. Our job is to take what you've got and build the best possible version of it, with your bottom line in mind.

02 · The money

Simple, and on your side.

Your moneyBanking is tied to your Shopify. Every customer payment lands in your account. We never sit between you and your sales.
ProductionYou front production at our actual cost: blanks, printing, freight, importing. What we pay is what you pay, no markup. Billed as goods are approved for production.
Our shareOn the 1st of each month, we invoice our 30% of product net, plus postage reimbursement at our exact cost.
03 · Built for pre-orders

Your customers fund the run.

Most of our clients run pre-orders, and that's by design. When you pre-sell, your customers' money funds production before we make a thing. No capital tied up in blanks, no warehouse full of stock that didn't move. We build to demand, not to a shelf.

04 · What's included

Clear lines. No surprises.

In the 30%

  • +Running your store & managing product lines
  • +Coordinating production end to end
  • +Warehousing active inventory
  • +Picking, packing & fulfilling orders
  • +Customer service: returns, exchanges & refunds, handled directly with your customers via your support inbox

Billed separately, at cost

  • ›Production & postage: passed through at our exact cost, never marked up.
  • ›Return shipping: passed through at our exact cost. Handling the return with your customer is part of your 30%.
  • ›Storage: free for the first 60 days while inventory moves. Stock that sits longer is billed at standard rates ($20/pallet or $3/bin per month). Most pre-order clients never see this.
  • ›Monthly minimum: a $250/month floor keeps your account active and supported. It only applies if your 30% share for the month comes in under it.
