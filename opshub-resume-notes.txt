⏺ Pushed (59f787e3) — the field now reads "Invoice # / ref (optional)" so it's
  clear you can skip it.

  The best reference for a CC-on-file vendor: the charge date. It's the one
  thing that's always there and lets you tie the line back to the card
  statement. So for Downeast, log 4277-A · 10,217.87 · ref "6/24" (or the charge
  date). Works today.

  But here's the "right way" worth doing [Likely], because it's more than a
  cosmetic label — it changes two things downstream:

  A CC charge isn't an invoice-to-pay; it's an already-paid expense. That
  matters in two places we're building toward:
  1. Open PO / cash flow — an invoice you've logged but not paid is still money 
  going out. A CC charge is already out. When we add paid-status (P4), CC
  charges should auto-count as paid, so they don't sit in the "billed-unpaid"
  bucket of your committed-cash number.
  2. QB push (P3) — you can't push a CC charge as a bill in QB (there's no bill
  to pay — it's a credit-card expense). So the method tells the push "record
  this as a CC expense, not an AP bill."

  So I'd add a small method to each line — Invoice / Credit card / Other —
  defaulting to Invoice. CC lines would show "CC · 6/24" instead of "inv —", and
  carry the paid/QB logic. And for a vendor like Downeast that always charges
  the card, I'd set Credit card as their default so your assistant never has to
  think about it — it's just pre-selected.

  That's a small schema add + a method picker on the log forms. Want me to build
  it now while you're on this vendor, or is the optional-ref enough for the
  testing pass and we batch it with P3/P4?
