Your role at a glance

You're the bridge between OpsHub and Shopify. Taylor scopes the pre-order. You build it in Shopify, manage it through the sale window, hand it to Drake for production, then handle inventory entry once it lands at the warehouse.

You also handle customer service — when a customer asks about their order, you look it up here.

Pre-order lifecycle (your view)

Each pre-order moves through phases. Each phase has an owner. You own Building in Shopify, you trigger Closed, and you handle Shopify entry during Fulfilling.

Planning
Taylor scopes products + lifecycle dates. You wait.
Building in Shopify
Your turn. Create each product in Shopify. Mark "Built in Shopify" per product as you finish.
Open · live
Pre-order is live. Customers buy. You monitor and answer questions.
Closed · pending push
Your turn. Pull the Shopify sold qtys report. Push to production.
Producing
Drake runs production in the linked Labs job. You wait.
Fulfilling
Your turn (with warehouse). Items arrive at HPD → you enter into Shopify → warehouse fulfills from ShipStation.
Complete
Done. Everything shipped.

Building products in Shopify Pre-order Detail · Building phase

Get the pre-order details from OpsHub
Open the pre-order (Ecomm → Pre-orders → click the project). You see:

Lifecycle dates — open date, close date, target ship date.
Products list — each product Taylor scoped: name, blank vendor, blank SKU, sizes, retail price, mockup file ID, notes.
Buffer % — the cushion that'll be applied at push time.
Notes — anything Taylor / Drake left for you.
Build each product in Shopify
  1. Go to the Shopify store for this client.
  2. Create a new product matching the OpsHub product. Use the mockup file as the product image (download from the product row).
  3. Set variants for each size. Use the retail price from OpsHub.
  4. For pre-orders, set Shopify to continue selling when out of stock (or use a pre-order app — depends on the client's store setup).
  5. Schedule the product to go live on the pre-order open date.
  6. Back in OpsHub, paste the Shopify product URL into the product row and check the "Built in Shopify" box. Timestamp records who and when.
  7. Repeat for every product.
Building progress shows as X/Y on the pre-order row in the Ecomm list. Once you've built everything, the list shows the pre-order is ready to go live.

Closing a pre-order Pre-order Detail · Open phase

When the sale window ends:

  1. Disable / unpublish the products in Shopify so no more orders come in.
  2. Pull the Sold by variant report from Shopify — qty per size per product.
  3. Back in OpsHub, on the pre-order detail page, click Close pre-order. Status moves to "Closed · pending push".
Drake will pick it up from there.

Pushing to production (handoff)

Drake runs the push-to-production wizard. It uses the buffer % from the pre-order. If you want to override the buffer for this specific run, you can edit it on the pre-order detail page before Drake pushes.

After push, the pre-order shows a linked Labs job in the Linked Job section. You can click through to see how production is going.

Entering inventory in Shopify /ecomm Intake tab

When inventory arrives at HPD
Warehouse receives the items. As soon as they Mark Received, the shipment appears on your Intake tab in /ecomm.

Each row = one shipment that contains stage-route items (i.e. inventory you need to enter into Shopify).

Click the row → modal opens with all eligible items, each as a checkbox. Items already entered into Shopify (or not eligible) are muted.
Enter the inventory in Shopify
  1. Go to the relevant Shopify store.
  2. For each product, update the inventory count to reflect what arrived (received qty minus samples).
  3. Back in OpsHub on the Intake modal, select the items you just entered (default = all selected).
  4. Click "Mark entered in Shopify". The bulk action logs activity, fires a phase recalc, and writes a timestamp.
Why this matters: The warehouse sees a green "Live in Shopify — shelve" section on /receiving for 48h after you mark items entered. That's their cue to physically organize the boxes onto shelves so ShipStation can pick from them.

Customer service lookups

When a customer asks "Where's my order?"
Most customer service questions come down to: where is this item, and when will it ship?

Two places to look:

1. Client Worksheet — Contacts → Clients → click the client → Worksheet tab. Filter by item status. Each row shows ETA, status, qty, retail price. Search by item name.

2. Pre-order Detail page — for pre-order customers, the pre-order's status tells you most of what you need. Open the linked Labs job for production details.
